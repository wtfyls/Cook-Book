<p>Lunchtime Cookbook is an in-game cookbook for Minecraft, made for modpacks with lots of food, farming and cooking content. It gives players a place to browse food and find out how to prepare it without having to rely on an external recipe menu.</p>

<h3>What it's for</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Browse food and cooking recipes in an in-game book.</li>
  <li>Keep recipes organized into simple categories.</li>
  <li>Show the ingredients and steps needed to make each item.</li>
  <li>Display different cooking methods, such as the furnace, skillet and stove.</li>
  <li>Work alongside recipe viewers such as JEI and EMI.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Development</h3>
<p>The mod is being developed with Minecraft 1.20.1 and Forge. Features and mod integrations are still being worked on, so the final feature set may change during development.</p>
